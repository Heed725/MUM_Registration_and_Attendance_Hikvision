# MUM logo assets

- `mum_logo.png`: the original logo supplied with the project (166 × 148 pixels).
- `mum_logo_hq.png`: high-resolution transparent PNG restoration used by the application header, favicon and reports (1331 × 1182 pixels).

The restored asset was produced with the built-in image generation tool from the official website’s emblem, preserving the university name, motto, book, graduation cap and green ribbon. It is an enhanced raster rendering, not an official vector master.

Reference: https://www.mum.ac.tz/themes/nexus/assets/img/logo1.png, accessed 10 November 2026.

Restoration prompt: Restore the official Muslim University of Morogoro emblem at high resolution with crisp edges, keeping its proportions, title “Muslim University of Morogoro”, motto “READ IN THE NAME OF ALLAH”, open book, purple graduation cap with yellow tassel, green disk and white/green ribbon. Preserve transparency outside the emblem and retain the original colours and arrangement; no additional elements or text.
